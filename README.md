# ⚡ Power Surge

A free idle RPG for your phone. Your hero fights on their own, and you level up, unlock powers, and keep grinding even while you're away.

**No ads. No purchases. No accounts.** Progress saves on your device.

## How to play

- **Your hero auto-battles.** Every 10 enemies clears a stage. Every 10th stage is a **boss**, and you have 30 seconds to beat it.
- **Tap the battle** to strike for extra damage.
- **Upgrades (gold):** Attack, HP, Attack Speed, Crit, Regen, Tap Damage, Greed. You can buy x1, x10, or Max.
- **Powers (skill points from hero levels):** Fireball, Lightning, Healing Light, Vampiric Edge, Frost Aura, Berserk, Iron Skin, Midas Touch. Active powers cast themselves.
- **Stuck on a boss?** You'll drop back and farm automatically. Tap **👑 Fight boss** when you're ready.
- **Ascend** (stage 25+): reset your run for **Souls**, which permanently give +10% damage & gold each.
- **Offline progress:** you keep earning gold and XP for up to 12 hours while away.
- **🎁 Free gift** every 2 hours.

## Play / install

Open `index.html` in a browser. On a hosted URL (e.g. GitHub Pages) it can be installed as an app:

- **iPhone:** Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android:** Chrome → ⋮ → *Install app*

Once installed it works offline.
